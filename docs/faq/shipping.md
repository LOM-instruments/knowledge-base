# Shipping

How and when orders ship, and import tax outside the EU. Package not delivered? See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#my-order-was-not-delivered).

## Timing

### When will my order be shipped?

All packages are usually shipped within 7 days of your order, unless noted otherwise (for example, an item sold as a pre-order). For full details, see the [shipping information page](https://store.lom.audio/pages/shipping-information).

## Outside the EU

### Shipping outside the EU and import tax

As a customer outside the European Union, you don't pay European VAT (around 20%, depending on the country). However, your courier or government may charge import tax and processing fees on delivery. We can't influence these fees — they're set by the courier company and your government.
