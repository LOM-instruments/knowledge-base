<h2>Key features</h2>
</div>

<div class="prose-block" markdown>

- Extended frequency response
- 60 dB maximum gain
- External input for additional sensors

<div class="banner dark">
<h2>How To Use Elektrosluch</h2>
</div>

<div class="prose-block" markdown>

1. Plug your headphones into the output
2. Turn on the device with the volume knob
3. Move the device with the sensors pointing towards the electromagnetic field source
4. Adjust the volume for optimal listening experience. Start with the volume low and be careful with your ears.
5. Enjoy!

</div>
