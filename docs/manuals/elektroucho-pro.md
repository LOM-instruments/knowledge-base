<div class="banner dark">
<h2>How To Use Elektroucho Pro</h2>
</div>

<div class="prose-block" markdown>

1. Make sure that phantom power on your input device is **off**
2. Plug Elektroucho Pro into the microphone input of your recorder or mixer
3. Adjust the gain accordingly and listen

!!! info
    In certain cases, you may notice Elektroucho Pro reacting when you touch it with your hands. This is expected — your body acts as an antenna that interacts with the device.
