Matched pair of miniature electret capsules on low-noise shielded cable.
Designed for ambient, binaural-style stereo, and point-source field
recording direct to portable recorders with plug-in power.

## Applications

- Field recording
- Binaural-style stereo
- Direct-to-recorder ambience

!!! note "Orientation tip"
    Matched omnis benefit from a small angular separation even though they
    are not directional. 17 cm spacing and 110° outward gives a wide,
    natural stereo image without the hollow centre of spaced pairs.
