# Useful links

A set of resources for field recordists — communities, sound libraries, and technical references.

## Communities & learning

- [Field Recording (Facebook group)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/field.recording/) — large, active community sharing recordings, gear, and technique.
- [LOM instruments (Facebook group)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/LOMlabel/) — the official LOM user community.
- [Wildlife Sound Recording Society](https://www.wildlife-sound.org/) — one of the oldest nature-recording societies; beginner guides and members' recordings.
- [Creative Field Recording](https://www.creativefieldrecording.com/) — long-running blog with gear reviews, technique articles, and interviews.

## Sound libraries & archives

- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) — huge collaborative library of Creative Commons sounds.
- [xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org/) — community database of bird and wildlife sounds from around the world.
- [Macaulay Library](https://www.macaulaylibrary.org/) — Cornell Lab's scientific archive of wildlife audio and video.
- [earth.fm](https://earth.fm/) — free streaming library of immersive nature soundscapes.

## Sound maps & projects

- [radio aporee ::: maps](https://aporee.org/maps/) — global collaborative soundmap of field recordings.
- [Cities and Memory](https://citiesandmemory.com/) — worldwide sound map pairing field recordings with reimagined compositions.

## Technique & reference

- [Selecting Mic Preamps](http://www.rane.com/note148.html) — microphone sensitivity, self-noise, and choosing preamps.
- [Sound System Interconnection](https://www.ranecommercial.com/legacy/note110.html) — building cable interconnects correctly.
- [Independent comparison of recorders](http://www.avisoft.com/recorder-tests) — measured noise figures for selecting low-noise field recorders.

## Software

- [Reaper](https://www.reaper.fm/) — affordable, lightweight DAW popular with field recordists.
- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/) — free, open-source audio editor.
